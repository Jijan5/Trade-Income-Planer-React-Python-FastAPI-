--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  What is Trade Income Planner (TIP)?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trade Income Planner (TIP) is a comprehensive FinTech SaaS platform designed
to help retail traders and beginners build the skills, discipline, and
emotional control required to trade profitably — before they risk real money
in the live markets.

The platform combines:
  - A fully simulated paper trading environment
  - AI-powered coaching and market analysis
  - Financial goal planning and compounding simulators
  - A social community hub for global trader discussions
  - Premium educational content (Learning Modules)
  - A complete admin system for full platform management

TIP bridges the gap between learning and live trading by giving users a safe,
structured environment to fail, learn, and grow without losing real capital.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2  Who is it built for?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Retail traders who are new to forex, crypto, or equities
  - Beginners who struggle with emotional control and revenge trading
  - Aspiring prop firm candidates who want to practice challenge rules
  - Intermediate traders who want to backtest strategies via simulation
  - Any trader looking for a community to share and discuss ideas

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3  The Origin Story
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This platform was built from personal experience. Like many beginners, the
original developer jumped into live trading without proper discipline or risk
management. The result was repeated revenge trading — chasing losses
emotionally — which ultimately led to losing over $1,000 (IDR 17,000,000+).

That loss was painful, but it sparked a clear mission: to build a platform
that forces traders to master emotional control and risk management in a
simulated environment BEFORE touching real money.

Every feature in this platform — from the Discipline Rules in the Manual Trade
Simulator, to the AI Trading Coach, to the Pre-Check Trade validation — was
designed specifically to solve the problems that caused that $1,000 loss.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4  Design Philosophy
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The platform's visual identity is inspired by the aesthetic of the video game
Cyberpunk 2077 — a dark, neon-glowing, futuristic interface that feels premium
and immersive. Research into trader communities also confirmed that the majority
of traders strongly prefer dark-themed interfaces for long trading sessions,
as they are easier on the eyes.

The Platform Appearance Engine (a Platinum feature) was created as a direct
response to mixed feedback from early testers who each had different aesthetic
preferences. Rather than picking one style and forcing it on everyone, the
"middle way" solution was to build a granular customization system that lets
each Platinum user set their own neon colors, glow intensities, glass opacity,
blur levels, and font families — with a live preview before applying.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This platform is sold as a 100% white-label product. See the dedicated
rebranding guide in Section 5.9 for step-by-step technical instructions on
how to fully rebrand the platform after acquisition.

2.1  Registration & Email Verification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To create an account on the platform:

  1. Click "Get Started" or "Sign Up" on the Landing Page.

3.1  Strategy Simulator
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Strategy Simulator lets you test trading strategies using mathematical
simulation without risking real capital. Real-time asset prices are fetched
via TradingView integration.

  SETTING UP A SIMULATION:
  Fill in the following parameters:
    - Initial Balance      : Your starting capital (e.g., $10,000)
    - Capital Utilization  : % of balance used per session
    - Risk Per Trade       : % of balance risked on each trade
    - Risk/Reward Ratio    : Your target RR (e.g., 2 = 1:2 RR)
    - Win Rate             : Expected % of winning trades
    - Trades Per Day       : How many trades you take daily
    - Simulation Days      : How many days to simulate (max 3,650)
    - Risk Type            : Dynamic (risk % of current balance) or
                             Fixed (risk % of starting balance)
    - Fees Per Trade       : Optional commission/spread cost per trade

  RUNNING THE SIMULATION:
  Click "Run Simulation". The backend engine iterates through each trade
  and calculates PnL outcomes based on your win rate, applying wins and
  losses to produce a day-by-day equity curve.

  READING THE RESULTS:
  - Equity curve chart showing balance growth over time
  - Total ROI (%)
  - Final Balance
  - Total trades won vs. lost
  - Detailed trade log

  WHAT-IF FUTURE PROJECTION:
  After running a simulation, use the What-If tool to project what your
  equity would look like if you continued trading with the same parameters
  into the future under different market scenarios.

  MONTE CARLO SIMULATION:
  The Monte Carlo tool runs hundreds of randomized variations of your
  strategy to show the statistical range of possible outcomes — best case,
  worst case, and most likely case. This helps you understand the true risk
  of your strategy before trading it live.

  SHARE STRATEGY:
  Once satisfied with a simulation, click "Share Strategy" to publish your
  exact parameters as a strategy card to the community feed. Other users
  can see your setup and copy it with one click.

  COPY STRATEGY:
  On the Community or Home Feed, if you see a shared strategy card from
  another user, click "Copy Strategy". The platform instantly loads their
  exact parameters into your Strategy Simulator form so you can run their
  simulation yourself.

  ACCESS: Premium and Platinum plans. Available during 7-day trial.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2  Goal Planner
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Goal Planner reverse-engineers the trading performance required to hit
a specific financial target within a given timeframe.

  HOW TO USE:
    1. Enter your Initial Balance (your current capital).
    2. Enter your Target Balance (how much you want to reach).
    3. Enter the Deadline in Months (how long you have to get there).
    4. Click "Calculate".

  OUTPUT:
    - Required Monthly Return (%): The consistent monthly gain you need
      to hit your goal via compounding.
    - Feasibility Assessment: The platform evaluates whether the required
      return is realistic (e.g., "Challenging but achievable",
      "Very Unlikely — reduce your goal or extend your deadline").
    - Month-by-month balance projection chart.

  This tool is especially useful for setting realistic trading expectations
  and avoiding the trap of over-leveraging to chase unrealistic goals.

  ACCESS: Premium and Platinum plans. Available during 7-day trial.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3  Manual Trade Simulator (Paper Trading)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Manual Trade Simulator is the most feature-rich section of the platform.
It is a full paper trading journal and simulator that enforces real trading
discipline on every trade you take.

  OPENING A PAPER TRADE:
    1. Navigate to the Manual Trade Simulator.
    2. Select your asset/symbol.
    3. Enter trade details: direction (Buy/Sell), entry price, stop loss,
       take profit, and position size.
    4. The built-in Risk Calculator immediately shows you the dollar amount
       at risk and your potential reward.
    5. Complete the Pre-Check Trade checklist before submitting.
    6. Click "Open Trade".

  PRE-CHECK TRADE:
  Before any trade can be submitted, the platform requires you to confirm
  a checklist of trading criteria (e.g., "Is this trade aligned with my
  plan?", "Have I set my stop loss?"). This simulates the mental discipline
  required in live trading and prevents impulsive entries.

  TRADE SETUP TEMPLATES:
  You can create and save reusable trade setup templates for your most
  common trade configurations. Instead of filling in all fields manually
  each time, load a template and your preferred settings are pre-filled.

  DISCIPLINE RULES:
  The platform allows you to configure personal discipline rules, such as:
    - Maximum number of trades per day
    - Maximum daily loss limit
    - Mandatory break after X consecutive losses
  If you violate a rule, the simulator blocks further trades for the session.
  This directly trains emotional control and prevents revenge trading.

  PROP FIRM CHALLENGE MODE:
  Simulate the exact conditions of a proprietary trading firm evaluation:
    - Set a Maximum Daily Drawdown limit
    - Set a Maximum Total Drawdown limit
    - Set a Profit Target
  The simulator tracks your progress against these rules and tells you if
  you have "passed" or "failed" the challenge — just like a real prop firm.

  AI TRADING COACH:
  After accumulating trade history, trigger the AI Trading Coach to analyze
  your trading behavior. Powered by Google Gemini, it evaluates:
    - Risk Management Score
    - Emotional Control Score (detecting revenge trading patterns)
    - System Adherence Score
  The AI provides specific, actionable feedback on where your trading
  psychology needs improvement.

  BUILT-IN RISK CALCULATOR:
  Integrated directly into the trade ticket. Enter your account balance,
  risk percentage, entry price, and stop loss — the calculator instantly
  shows your exact position size and dollar risk before you commit.

  SHORT TUTORIALS & GLOSSARY:
  Beginner help widgets are embedded throughout the simulator. Hover over
  any metric (Win Rate, Drawdown, Risk/Reward, etc.) to see a plain-English
  explanation. A full Glossary is also accessible from the simulator.

  ACCESS: All plans (Free and above).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2  Dashboard Overview
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first screen shows a bird's-eye view of the platform's health:

  - TOTAL USERS      : Total registered user accounts

  - MRR              : Monthly Recurring Revenue in USD (calculated from

5.6  Security Architecture
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  JWT AUTHENTICATION:
    - All protected endpoints require a valid JWT Bearer token.
    - Tokens expire after 30 minutes (configurable via .env).
    - Tokens are issued on successful login.

  PASSWORD HASHING:
    - Passwords are hashed using bcrypt + SHA256 for enhanced security.
    - Plain-text passwords are NEVER stored in the database.

  EMAIL VERIFICATION (REGISTRATION):
    - On registration, a unique secure token is generated and emailed.
    - The user's account is marked "unverified" until the link is clicked.
    - Unverified accounts are hard-blocked from receiving JWT tokens.
    - This prevents bots from abusing the 7-day free trial.

  PIN-BASED SENSITIVE UPDATES:
    - Changing password (from profile) requires a 6-digit PIN sent via email.
    - The PIN has a 15-minute expiration stored server-side.
    - This means a session hijacker cannot change credentials without
      also having access to the user's email inbox.

  RATE LIMITING:
    - 60 requests per minute per IP address (enforced via slowapi).
    - Exceeding the limit returns HTTP 429 Too Many Requests.

  CORS:
    - Configurable allowed origins via the .env file.
    - Only whitelisted domains can make cross-origin API requests.

  SECURITY HEADERS:
    - X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
    - X-Frame-Options: DENY
    - X-XSS-Protection: 1; mode=block
    - Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS)

  SQL INJECTION PREVENTION:
    - All database queries use SQLModel's parameterized query system.
    - Raw SQL is only used in specific admin delete operations with
      explicit parameter binding (no string interpolation).

  ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC):
    - Two roles: "user" and "admin".
    - Admin-only endpoints are protected by the get_current_admin_user
      dependency which raises HTTP 403 for non-admin callers.

  DATA SANITIZATION:
    - User-generated HTML content is sanitized with DOMPurify on the
      frontend before rendering to prevent XSS attacks.

5.7  Payment Integration (LemonSqueezy / Stripe)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CURRENT PROVIDER: LemonSqueezy

  HOW IT WORKS:
    1. User clicks "Upgrade" and is redirected to a LemonSqueezy
       checkout URL (generated by the backend).
    2. User completes payment on LemonSqueezy's hosted checkout page.
    3. LemonSqueezy sends a signed webhook POST request to:
           POST /api/payment/webhook
    4. The backend verifies the webhook signature using the
       LEMONSQUEEZY_WEBHOOK_SECRET from .env.
    5. On a successful "order_created" or "subscription_created" event,
       the backend automatically updates the user's plan in the database.
    6. The user's new features are available on their next page load.

  WEBHOOK ENDPOINT:
    File: backend/app/routers/payment.py
    Route: POST /api/payment/webhook

  SWITCHING TO STRIPE (FOR NEW OWNER):
  The webhook handler is isolated in payment.py. A developer can replace
  LemonSqueezy with Stripe by:
    1. Replacing the LemonSqueezy SDK calls with the Stripe Python SDK.
    2. Updating the webhook signature verification logic (Stripe uses
       stripe.Webhook.construct_event() instead of LemonSqueezy's method).
    3. Replacing the .env keys with Stripe's API key and webhook secret.
    4. Updating the checkout URL generation logic to use Stripe's
       Payment Links or Checkout Sessions API.
  The rest of the platform (plan enforcement, user model) requires no changes.
